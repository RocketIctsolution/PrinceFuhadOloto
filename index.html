About Prince Fuhad

A prince who chose to build, not just inherit

Prince Fuhad Oloto is a member of the Oloto royal family of Lagos and one of the city's most recognisable young business figures — known equally in boardrooms and at the heart of Lagos's social and cultural life.

As Chairman and CEO of the Futol Group, he oversees a portfolio of companies spanning construction, logistics, private security, environmental services, residential real estate and hospitality — each built with the same discipline and attention to detail he brings to his royal responsibilities.

"A man is made not by age, but by pain, failure, rejection and the risks he's willing to take."

Beyond the boardroom, Prince Fuhad remains deeply connected to his royal heritage and community — a steady presence at cultural, philanthropic and family milestones, and an advocate for the values that shaped him.
